Attention

Attention = each word takes its query card (Q), compares it with every word’s key card (K), then mixes their value cards (V) in proportion.

Why it’s designed this way: “Whom to look at” isn’t a hard-coded rule but learned from data; all words can be computed at once, so it’s fast.

▶ Open the interactive version (tap the diagram, try labs, take the quiz)

🧒 In plain words

Attention is like finding books in a library: you hold a question (the query card Q), compare it with the label on each book’s spine (the key card K), and the better a book matches, the more carefully you read its contents (the value card V). Every word “searches” the other words of the sentence this way.

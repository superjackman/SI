Training & RL

The skills aren’t written, they’re trained.

The skills aren’t written, they’re trained: first self-study “guess the next word” on huge amounts of text, then learn formats from demonstrations, and finally polish with rewards and preferences.

Why it’s designed this way: Rules can never be written out in full, but examples and feedback can be unlimited; let the model work out the patterns from data itself.

▶ Open the interactive version (tap the diagram, try labs, take the quiz)

🧒 In plain words

A model’s skills are not written in rule by rule; they are practised, like learning to talk: first listen to a huge amount and work things out yourself (self-study), then copy a teacher’s examples (imitation), and finally get praise when right and corrections when wrong (rewards). Tap the boxes to see each stage.

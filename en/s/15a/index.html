Multimodal

VLMs, diffusion, speech

▶ Open the interactive version (diagrams, labs, quizzes)

The problem Understand and generate images and speech, not only text.

Why care

The world is more than text: screenshots, photos, speech.

Everyday analogy

  • Patch images into tokens that share attention with text.
  • Diffusion washes an image out of noise.
  • Speech becomes audio tokens too.

Core idea

  • Vision-language models: image patches as tokens (ViT). Vision encoder → projection → language model.
  • Diffusion: add noise step by step, learn to denoise; generation starts from noise with text guidance.
  • Speech: spectrograms or discrete audio tokens; the same attention can handle them.

What the picture shows

  • Image → patches → visual tokens → Transformer with text.

The numbers

  • 224×224 / 16 → tokens (same as KA07).
  • A 224 × 224 screenshot becomes 196 visual tokens (same as card KA07), concatenated with text tokens into the Transformer.

Formula

  • loss = ‖ε − ε_θ(x_t, t)‖²
  • ViT: visual tokens
  • Diffusion objective: loss = ‖ε − ε_θ(x_t, t)‖²
  • ViT patches: visual tokens
  • Same attention, new modalities

Left open It can see. Hard problems still want it to think longer when answering; long documents want a wider window.

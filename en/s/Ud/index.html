Use it well

Brief clearly, manage context, verify.

With the same agent, results swing on how clearly you brief it and how clean the context is: say the four things, give what is relevant, plan, verify, keep safe habits.

Why it’s designed this way: The model only sees what you give it; these habits all help it see the right things.

▶ Open the interactive version (tap the diagram, try labs, take the quiz)

🧒 In plain words

The helper only sees what you give it; it cannot guess what you meant but did not say. Like asking someone to shop for you: “get some food” versus “what to buy, who it is for, the budget, and when to deliver” gives very different results.

📑 What this stop covers

  • Same agent, very different results
    The model sees only what you give it, never the requirements you kept in your head. So the key is on your side:
  • ✍️ Say four things clearly
    When giving a task, make four things clear: the result you want, the background, what must not be touched, and what counts as done. Adding one example often helps more than extra…
  • 🧹 Manage the context
    The helper’s “desk” (its context) is limited in size, and the fuller it gets, the easier it is for important things to get buried. So: one topic per chat; tell it where a file is…
  • 🔍 Verify the result
    “Done” from the helper does not mean really done. First have it check its own work (run tests, compare with the requirements), then spot-check the important parts yourself…
  • 🛡️ Safe habits
    A few good habits: start with few keys and take them back afterwards; always approve sending, deleting and paying yourself; web pages and emails it reads may hide bad…
  • 📋 An acceptance checklist for what comes back
    What comes back is like homework handed in, to be marked against a checklist: for code, check the tests really passed; for reports, open a few sources; for tables, check the…

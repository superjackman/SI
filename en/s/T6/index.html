Multimodal

Seeing, hearing and drawing all use the same trick.

Seeing, hearing and drawing all use the same trick: first turn images and sound into tokens / vectors just like text and hand them to the same LLM; on the generation side, turn vectors back again.

Why it’s designed this way: Reusing an already strong language model and training only an “adapter” is far cheaper than building a model from scratch for every modality.

▶ Open the interactive version (tap the diagram, try labs, take the quiz)

🧒 In plain words

A large model only knows text at first. To let it see pictures and hear sounds, we first translate them into “building blocks” it knows (a string of numbers, just like text), then hand them over together. To make it draw or speak, the blocks are assembled back into pictures and sound. Tap the boxes in the diagram to see each step.

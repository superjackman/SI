Math · Linear algebra

Vectors → matrices → determinant → Jacobian: what one “layer” actually computes.

The linear algebra a neural network uses, with step-by-step proofs of the dot product’s geometry, the transpose rule, the area formula and the transpose rule for gradients.

Why it’s designed this way: These few rules are what you need to read the formulas for a layer and for attention.

▶ Open the interactive version (tap the diagram, try labs, take the quiz)

🧒 In plain words

Computers don’t understand words, only numbers. So an AI turns each word into a list of numbers, like describing a person by three numbers: height, weight and age. Such a list is called a vector.

One layer of a neural network mostly multiplies that list by a table of numbers (a matrix) to get a new list. So the vectors and matrices on this stop are what an AI computes all the time.

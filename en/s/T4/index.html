Transformer block

Each Transformer block does two things.

Each Transformer block does two things: first words “look at” each other (attention), then each word “digests” on its own (FFN); residuals and normalization keep dozens of stacked layers trainable.

Why it’s designed this way: Separating “exchanging information” from “processing individually” keeps the structure simple, endlessly stackable, and well suited to GPU parallelism.

▶ Open the interactive version (tap the diagram, try labs, take the quiz)

🧒 In plain words

In the middle of a large model sit many identical “blocks” stacked up like building bricks. Each brick does just two things: first the words look at each other (attention), then each word thinks on its own (the feed-forward network). After each, the new idea is added onto the old one and the “volume” is evened out.

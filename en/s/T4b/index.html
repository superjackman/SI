FFN & output

The FFN expands, filters and shrinks each word’s vector, like looking up a “knowledge dictionary”; the final output scoring layer scores every word in the vocabulary, and softmax turns the scores into probabilities.

Why it’s designed this way: Attention only “moves” information; the real non-linear processing and knowledge storage happen mostly in the FFN.

▶ Open the interactive version (tap the diagram, try labs, take the quiz)

🧒 In plain words

After the meeting, each word goes back to its desk to think: its list of numbers is first blown up into lots of little “detectors”, each recognising one pattern; a “switch” keeps lit only the detectors that found something; then the lit signals are added up and shrunk back to the original length.

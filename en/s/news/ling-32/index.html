Ant Group released Ling-3.2-flash on 5 Nov 2026: 560 billion…

2026-11-05 · TechNode · 2026-11-02

Ant Group released Ling-3.2-flash on 5 Nov 2026: 560 billion parameters in total, about 25 billion used on each step, a one-million-token context, and text, image and video input.

The source states 560 billion parameters in total and about 25 billion used on a step: that step does not touch the whole table.

Stop 12, “only a few experts wake up”, is that arrangement: most parameters sleep for the step.

It also reads images and video. Stop 15a cuts a picture into patches and lines them up with the words.

The context is listed as one million tokens. Stop 15b separates “it fits” from “it thinks longer”: a longer window is not the same as using every token in the middle.

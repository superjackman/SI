App overview

You say one sentence, and six layers relay it behind the scenes.

You say one sentence, and six layers relay it behind the scenes: the agent program orchestrates and acts, the LLM does the “thinking” and “writing”, training builds the skills offline, and compute makes it all run.

Why it’s designed this way: Each layer does one job: the model can be swapped, tools added, training redone, without affecting each other.

Let it run alone

Hand it off; it runs to the end; you check afterwards.

Background / cloud agents, routines and multi-agent teams: nobody watches, so safety comes from isolation, checkable output, and a person pressing the final button.

Why it’s designed this way: Clear, checkable work does not need you present the whole time; save your time for review and decisions.

▶ Open the interactive version (tap the diagram, try labs, take the quiz)

🧒 In plain words

“Letting it work on its own” is like handing a job to someone and checking it later. Nobody is watching, so three things keep it safe: it works somewhere it cannot break anything; what it hands back can be checked; and the step that cannot be undone (merging, sending, paying) is left for you to press.

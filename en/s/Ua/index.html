Work alongside it

You watch; it does the work step by step.

Chat assistants, coding agents in the editor, command-line agents and computer-use agents: you start it, you watch, you approve key steps.

Why it’s designed this way: Work whose goal is still moving and needs your decisions fits “alongside” best: you catch a mistake at the first step.

▶ Open the interactive version (tap the diagram, try labs, take the quiz)

🧒 In plain words

“Working together” is like having an apprentice at your side: you ask, you watch, you see every step, and it does the important things only after you say yes. Of the four ways, each can touch more than the one before, so the further down, the more carefully you manage its “keys”.

LLM

An LLM is a “guess the next word” machine.

An LLM is a “guess the next word” machine: text cut into tokens → turned into vectors → dozens of layers of processing → every candidate word scored → one drawn and appended, over and over.

Why it’s designed this way: “Guess the next word” exercises can be generated automatically from any text, so it can be trained on huge amounts of data.

▶ Open the interactive version (tap the diagram, try labs, take the quiz)

🧒 In plain words

A large model is really playing a word-chain game: you give it a beginning, it guesses “what is the most likely next word”, adds it, then guesses the next… writing a whole passage one word at a time.
